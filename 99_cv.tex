\openright\chapter{Curriculum Vitae}

In 2014, Tom Westerhout started his Bachelor's program at Radboud University in Nijmegen, Netherlands. During his undergraduate studies, he assisted in teaching courses for the Physics and Computing Science departments. In 2017, he completed a Google Summer of Code program with Boost.org and defended his Bachelor's thesis titled "Plasmons in fractals". 

From 2017 to 2020, Tom Westerhout pursued his Master's studies at Radboud University, during which he completed another Google Summer of Code program with Boost.org, an internship at the programming languages research group at the University of Coimbra in Portugal, an Erasmus+ internship at Saint-Petersburg State University in Russia, and an internship at the Center for Computational Quantum Physics, Flatiron Institute, in New York, US. In 2020, he defended his Master's thesis titled "Did we forget a phase? VUMPS study of the t-V-V' model in one dimension". 

Between 2020 and 2025, Tom Westerhout worked as a PhD student at the Institute for Molecules and Materials, Radboud University, under the supervision of prof.\,dr. M.\,I. Katsnelson. During his PhD, he visited the Computational Quantum Science Lab at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne in Switzerland, completed an internship with Tweag, participated in a student researcher program with Google Quantum AI in Santa Barbara, US, and did an internship with Optiver in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Tom Westerhout currently works as a Research Scientist at Google Quantum AI.
